About Argo Data

Temperature: Sea temperature measured on the ITS-90 scale in degrees Celsius
Salinity: Practical salinity measured in PSU (Practical Salinity Units)
Pressure: Sea water pressure in decibar (0 at sea level)
Cycles: Float cycle numbers (0 = launch, 1+ = complete cycles)

Query Tips

Ask for specific ranges: "temperature between 10-20°C"
Request trends: "salinity changes over time"
Compare data: "temperature vs depth"
Get statistics: "average, min, max values"
